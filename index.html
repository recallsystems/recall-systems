The 90-second demo

Watch a missed call become a booked appointment.

This is the entire product, end to end — the same sequence we run live on a sales call:

  1. 0:00 — The call. We dial the clinic's number from a mobile and let it ring out, exactly as a patient would at 7:42pm. On screen: the call timer, the clinic's real number.
  2. 0:15 — The text-back. Within 60 seconds, the missed caller receives an SMS from the clinic's number: name, apology, one question. On screen: the actual message arriving on the phone.
  3. 0:35 — The conversation. The "patient" replies: "Toothache — tomorrow morning if possible." The system asks one clarifying question (new or existing patient), then offers two real open slots pulled from the calendar.
  4. 1:00 — The booking. Patient picks 9:20am. The appointment lands in the clinic's calendar; the patient gets a confirmation text. On screen: the calendar entry appearing in real time.
  5. 1:20 — The staff alert. The practice manager gets a summary: who called, what they needed, what was booked — no action required unless it's flagged urgent.
  6. 1:30 — The math. One recovered call: ~$250 of immediate revenue (Patient Prism 2026). The build costs less than twenty such calls a year.

Demo uses a test number and test calendar — no real patients are ever contacted during demonstrations.
